Quantum Arena evaluates how well AI models turn quantum programming tasks into code. Our initial work benchmarks general-purpose language models and quantum-specific coding assistants on Qiskit code generation.

Our starting point

From a prompt to working quantum code

We bring together quantum programming datasets, saved model outputs, and evaluation results in one place. Compare performance in the leaderboard, then explore individual problems and generated code to see where models succeed and where they still struggle.

Where we are going

A shared task for the quantum community

01

A shared evaluation task

Our goal is to give researchers a common set of tasks and transparent evaluation methods for measuring progress in quantum code generation.

02

A living leaderboard

We invite the community to submit models and datasets to our team. The leaderboard is intended to grow with new contributions, tasks, and evaluations.

03

All quantum languages

We are starting with Qiskit and aim to support quantum programming across languages and frameworks, including PennyLane and beyond.

About us

Hosted by the Quantum Science Center

Quantum Arena is a QSC hosted leaderboard for exploring AI-assisted quantum programming. We aim to connect model developers, quantum researchers, and dataset contributors through shared evaluation.
