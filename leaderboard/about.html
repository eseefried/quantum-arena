About Us

The team behind Quantum Arena

Quantum Arena is a Quantum Science Center hosted leaderboard focused on AI for quantum code generation. Our goal is to build a shared evaluation resource that grows with community contributions and expands across quantum programming languages and frameworks.

Project Leads

Ethan Seefried

Project Team Lead

About: Ethan is a postdoctoral researcher in the Quantum Science Center working under Tirthankar Ghosal. He earned his PhD from Colorado State University in 2026, specializing in computer vision. His current research focuses on machine learning for quantum computing and computer vision for cancer research. He has been at Oak Ridge National Laboratory for one year and is excited to help advance quantum computing. Outside of research, Ethan shares his home with his two cats, Griff and Luna, who have been with him since his first year of undergrad.
